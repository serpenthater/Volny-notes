Is the highest position one can have aside from being a saint in the church, usually reserved for advanced clergy and leaders of monasteries. The title of [[Revered Father of the House]] in [[Volnygorod]] belongs to [[Yudif Shinya]]. 

The title exists out of two terms "Father of the House" meaning leader of a monestary
Revered meaning seniority

Some other terms that people use instead of this one are: Archsufferer", "Exalted Sufferer", "Master Sufferer", and "Enduring Servant". Yet the [[Revered Father of the House]] prefers to keep his name humble for public appearance.
