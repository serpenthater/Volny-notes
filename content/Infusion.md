Is a ROMOURED process with which a soul of a powerful entity merges with a humanoid. This can only be done with the help of some unknown power of which many rumors are spread. Yet none of them have a concrete set in stone method of achieving this process. It changes rumor by rumor, for some its an organization, for others its a magic item, for some others its a powerful deity turning on its own kin. Yet two  thing stays the same. 

First the end results of a power humanoid warlock who has not given up his soul for this power and has powers far beyond a regular warlock. That being far more powerful magic and some form of physical transformation. Be it majestic wings for those who merged with dragons or some archfey, to holy aura's that force all to speak the truth in front of them. However its not clear how much control these infused souls have over these transformations. Maybe they are able to turn these traits off at will or maybe its always part of them.   

A second thing that never changes is the location. Namely this always happens in [[Volnygorod]], the proper district does change from time to time but the city never does. With the most common mentioned district being [[Azartnya Fortuna]]. With such a clear and consistent location it would have been easy enough for those powerful entities to solve the problem. Yet here the problem arises, there is some kind of barrier? Spell? Ritual? Holding them from interacting with the city in direct ways. The best they can do for now is to send their personal warlocks to fix or find a solution for the issue. Yet non of these warlocks have returned successful and the reasons why arent clear either...
