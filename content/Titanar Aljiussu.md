Is a Nymph [[Infusion|infused]] with the soul of a high fey. Due to this powerful soul stuck in him, he has a high magical attunement and is able to cast spells without thinking much about it.
#### Personality 

Titanar  is a man of leisure who often finds himself going with the flow rather than fighting against the rising tide. Unlike [[Yudif Shinya]] or [[Dusha Waiba]], who both take the [[Gangs]] seriously, Titanar views it as a way to revel in living the high life ( gambling, women, fine liquor, etc.). He expresses boredom at formal meetings. That does not make him any less committed to his seat as a leader of the [[Glass thorns]]

However, beneath his laconic exterior is a cunning man, skilled in gathering information and making good on threats. Being an extortion specialist, this is expected of him. In comparison to [[Yudif Shinya]] brutishness, he appeals to reason. After being refused, then he becomes hostile and threatening. His mood, thus, appears capable of changing rapidly, which he uses to increase his fear factor. He is also extremely sadistic, killing people for intimidation, despite just having fun with them, and how he joined the [[Gangs]] in the first place because he liked the feeling of beating a guy's face. Thus, there is far more than meets the eye with Titanar, as his unserious exterior reveals a man capable of extreme cruelty if it serves his purposes.

There is a tragic component to Titanar's story, as he reveals his reason for becoming a hedonist - his inferiority complex with [[Leader]]. At one point, he was a strong contender for a leadership role in the [[Gangs]], but gave up when the going got tough, content with worldly comforts instead of striving for the top. Shame as he had the potential to be so much more. Titanar is self-aware about this.

In the [[Gangs]],  he appears to be close to [[Yudif Shinya]] especially, often gambling with him when out-of-hours and sending a few mocking comments his way
