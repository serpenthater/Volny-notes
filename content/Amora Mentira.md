
#### Personality

Reina is a kind and caring woman who considers [[Lando Lordo]] and [[Hero]] as friends. She was nothing short of supportive and always sought to help them even when the circumstances were dire. Throughout the years, she harbored deep unrequited feelings of love for [[Titanar Aljiussu]] , so much so that she was willing to sell [[Hero]] out just because she loved [[Titanar Aljiussu]] This however, proved to be her downfall, despite her remorse and attempt to atone for what she had done.
