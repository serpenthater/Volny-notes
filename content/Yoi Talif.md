Secret leader of the [[Gangs]], he is the one who controls the most upper level workings of the [[Gangs]]. The gang leaders listen to him and his words as if its law. Paying their dues to him and making sure they stay in his good graces. Yet no one is able to pin down who exactly he could be, only hearing his commands trough varying means. 

#### Personality

The biggest personality trait Yoi shows is his obsession with secrecy; emphasizing the erasure of any trace of his history and keeping his identity a secret to assume his position of power as a compulsion he will fulfill by any means necessary. As he believes the mistakes of the past, or simply the past itself, are something to be defeated and destroyed for human beings to grow, he is driven anxiously about his past being a threat to him, linking it to fear, and being exceptionally apprehensive of anyone learning of him. Even in the [[Gangs]] hierarchy. The only exception being [[Lando Lordo]] as he holds a soft spot towards him due to their years of working together as partners, as well as viewing him as no threat due to his pathetic demeaner.

His second most prominent personality trait is his pride and confidence. This comes due to the [[Infusion]] he has inside of him namely that of a demi-god. Granting him near unimaginable power when its needed, yet he does not use it unless he is pushed to his wits end as his obsessions with secrecy always trumps over him. Not wanting people to know what he is and what he can do. His pride will prevail and he will constantly choose to directly confront whatever problem he has.
