World is set in various realms, each based on the ancestry of the realms in it. I.E a realm for humans and their varients, a realms for elfs, a realm for dwarves. 

The more common an ancestry is the bigger the realm. Due to this less common ancestry either have their own small realms stuck between bigger realms (Half elves) or on the far edges of a realm. (Most monsters fall under this part). Or they make the big realms as their suzerain like dwarfs and gnomes.

The larger realms are called:
[[Emberi]] realms of the humans
[[Epral]] realm of the [[elves]]
[[Oshuylor]] realm of the [[dwarves]]
[[Zoltosmey]] realm of the [[dragonborns]]
